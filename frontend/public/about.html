01 — Origin

A name chosen for what it demands of us.

The Derby horse is not admired for speed alone. It is admired because speed means nothing without discipline, and strength means nothing without temperament. We took the name because it sets a standard we have to keep earning.

Medicine is a promise. When a distributor stocks our product, a pharmacist recommends it, and a family finally affords it — that chain only works if not a single link decides "close enough" is good enough.

— Derby Lifescience, Faridabad
