About Derby Lifescience

Medicine you can rely on, priced so people can reach it

Derby Lifescience is a pharmaceutical company built on a simple belief: quality healthcare should never be a privilege. We develop, source and distribute a broad range of medicines, from pain management and anti-infectives to diabetes care and nutraceuticals.

We combine manufacturing depth with a distribution network designed for speed, so a pharmacy in a Tier-3 town waits no longer than one in a metro.

Every batch is tested before it ships. Every price is set so treatment stays within reach. That commitment to quality and patient care is the whole company.
